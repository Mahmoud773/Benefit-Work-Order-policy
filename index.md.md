Privacy Policy for Benefit Wo

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Benefit Wo (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a work-order and facility-management application for malls and similar sites. It is used by clients, technicians, mall staff, managers, accountants, and administrators to create, track, inspect, execute, pay for, and close work orders.

The Benefit Wo mobile app is free to download. Some work-order services (such as inspection or execution fees) may require payment. Those payments are for the facility service itself. They are not Google Play in-app purchases.

If you use the app, you agree to this Privacy Policy. We use personal information only to provide and improve the service, and we share it only as described here.

Information we collect

Depending on your role and how you use the app, we may collect:

Account details: name, email address, password (handled by our authentication provider), user role, and the mall or site you are linked to.
Work-order data: descriptions, statuses, schedules, reports, comments, ratings, and related business fields needed to run the order.
Photos and camera images: photos of issues, inspection or execution reports, and payment receipts (for example InstaPay receipts) that you or staff upload.
Payment records we store: amount, payment method (card via Paymob, cash, or InstaPay), payment status, and related timestamps. We do not store your full card number, CVV, or card PIN. Card details are entered on Paymob’s payment pages.
Notifications: a device token so we can send push notifications about your orders and account.
Device and technical data: device type, operating system, app version, crash or performance data, and identifiers needed to operate the app, send notifications, show ads, and reduce fraud. This is not used to sell a profile of you as a private individual outside the service.
We do not collect GPS or live device location. A mall or site name you or an administrator enter is a workplace label, not phone location. We do not collect gender unless you later provide it yourself in a field we add; the current service does not require gender.

How we use information

We use this information to:

Create and manage accounts and roles.
Operate work orders (assignment, inspection, execution, confirmation, complaints, closing).
Store and display photos and reports to the parties who need them.
Process and confirm payments and accounting for those orders.
Send service notifications.
Secure the app, fix errors, and improve performance.
Show ads (see Advertising below).
Who can see your information

Work-order information (including photos) is visible to users who are authorized for that order or that mall, such as the client, assigned staff, mall customer service, maintenance or operations managers, accountants, and administrators, according to their role. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

We use third parties that process data on our behalf to run the app:

Google Firebase (including authentication, cloud database, file storage, cloud messaging / push notifications, and performance monitoring).
Google AdMob (advertising, including app-open ads). This may use an advertising identifier.
Paymob (card payments). Card data is processed by Paymob according to Paymob’s own policy.
These providers may process data on servers outside your country. They may access personal information only to perform their tasks for us. They are not permitted to use it for their own unrelated purposes, except as required by their own legal terms (for example advertising measurement by AdMob).

You can review:

Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Paymob: the privacy policy published on Paymob’s website
The app does not use website cookies itself. Advertising and analytics SDKs may use cookies or similar technologies on their side. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Google advertising / tracking settings). Some features still work if you limit ads; ads may be less relevant.

Permissions

The Android app may request:

Internet and network state, to run the service.
Camera, to capture work-order, report, or receipt photos.
Notifications, to alert you about orders and account events.
We do not request device location permission.

Payments

If you pay by card, you complete payment on Paymob. We receive confirmation and store payment status and amount on the work order. Cash and InstaPay are recorded in the app according to your and staff actions (including receipt images where used).

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including Firebase access controls and role-based access in the app). No internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Benefit Wo is a professional workplace app. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete the information.
